# 5 Days AI Learning Plan

## Day 1: Introduction to AI
- Understand what AI is
- Learn about the history and evolution of AI
- Explore different types of AI

## Day 2: Machine Learning Basics
- Introduction to machine learning concepts
- Types of machine learning: supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement
- Hands-on with simple ML algorithms

## Day 3: Deep Learning and Neural Networks
- Understand neural networks
- Explore deep learning frameworks
- Build a simple neural network model

## Day 4: AI Tools and Frameworks
- Overview of popular AI tools
- Hands-on with TensorFlow/PyTorch
- Training and evaluating AI models

## Day 5: AI Applications and Ethics
- Explore real-world AI applications
- Discuss ethical considerations in AI
- Future of AI and career paths
