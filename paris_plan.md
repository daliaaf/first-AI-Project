# 3-Day Trip to Paris

## Day 1: Classic Paris
- Morning: Visit the Eiffel Tower and enjoy the view from the top.
- Afternoon: Explore the Champs-Élysées and visit the Arc de Triomphe.
- Evening: Dinner at a traditional French bistro near the Seine River.

## Day 2: Art and History
- Morning: Visit the Louvre Museum and see famous artworks like the Mona Lisa.
- Afternoon: Walk through the historic Marais district and visit the Picasso Museum.
- Evening: Enjoy a Seine river cruise to see Paris illuminated.

## Day 3: Culture and Leisure
- Morning: Explore Montmartre and visit the Sacré-Cœur Basilica.
- Afternoon: Relax in Luxembourg Gardens and visit nearby cafes.
- Evening: Attend a show at the Moulin Rouge or a local theater.
