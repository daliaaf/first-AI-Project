NYC Transportation Plan for 5 Days Trip - Detailed

1. Subway Routes and Tips:
- To visit Times Square: Take the 1, 2, 3, N, Q, R, or W trains to Times Sq-42nd St Station.
- For Central Park: Use the N, R, W to 5th Ave/59th St or 1, 2, 3 to 72nd or 81st St stations.
- Financial District: Use the 4, 5, J, Z trains to Wall St or Fulton St stations.
- Brooklyn Bridge Park: Take the 2 or 3 trains to Clark St or Court St.
- Example route: From JFK Airport, take the AirTrain to Jamaica Station, then subway E line to Manhattan.

2. Taxis and Ride-Sharing:
- Yellow cabs can be hailed on the street or via apps.
- Uber and Lyft coverage is extensive; prices vary based on distance and demand.
- For late-night trips, taxis and ride-shares are safer than waiting for subways.

3. Walking Paths:
- Times Square to Central Park is approximately 1.5 miles (30 minutes walk).
- Central Park loop walk is about 6 miles; shorter routes available.
- Financial District walking tours cover Wall St, the 9/11 Memorial, and nearby attractions.

Use a transit app or MTA website for real-time subway updates and walking navigation apps for the best routes.
