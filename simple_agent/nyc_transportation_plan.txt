NYC Transportation Plan for 5 Days Trip

1. Subway Routes:
- Use the MTA Subway system for efficient travel. Key lines include 1, 2, 3 (Broadway), A, C, E (8th Ave), N, Q, R, W (Broadway), and the 4, 5, 6 (Lexington Ave).
- Subway operating hours are 24/7 but schedules vary.

2. Taxis:
- Yellow cabs are readily available in Manhattan.
- Use ride-sharing apps like Uber and Lyft for convenience.

3. Walking Paths:
- NYC is very walkable, especially in Manhattan.
- Walking is recommended for areas like Times Square, Central Park, and the Financial District.
